# Crestwood visual system
Inherited from the implemented reference-led design. A premium school prospectus for families: large editorial typography, deep navy structural elements, sky-blue learning surfaces and amber key moments. Natural Nigerian student photography, roomy layouts and a distinct rounded photographic corner.

Manrope display; DM Sans body; self-hosted. Body 16px minimum. Fluid heading scale. Clear contrast, focus rings, semantic HTML, native dialog and disclosures. Interactive press feedback 160ms. Curriculum tabs switch immediately. Gentle scroll reveals once, disabled for reduced motion. Mobile navigation uses a named 48px control and Escape close. Two-column educational content stacks on narrow screens.

No fabricated outcomes or testimonials. Brand and programmes are sample content pending school information.
